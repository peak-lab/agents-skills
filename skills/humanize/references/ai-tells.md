# AI Tells

Detection catalogue for `humanize`. Load it for long-form prose, or when the quick scan flags a real issue and the right rewrite is unclear. A trigger is a signal, not a verdict: rewrite the pattern, not the token.

## Inflated significance

Triggers: serves as, marks a turning point, stands as a testament to, plays a crucial or pivotal role, underscores its importance, reflects a broader landscape, lasting legacy, cornerstone, paves the way for. French: constitue, marque un tournant, témoigne de, joue un rôle clé, souligne l'importance, s'inscrit dans une dynamique plus large, pierre angulaire.

The tell upgrades a fact into a symbol. Delete the upgrade and keep the fact.

## Trailing analysis

Triggers: highlighting, ensuring, showcasing, reflecting, contributing to, fostering, underscoring, demonstrating. French: mettant en avant, garantissant, illustrant, reflétant, contribuant à, soulignant.

Cut a participle clause bolted on to add depth, or make it a sentence with a real subject.

## Promotional register

Triggers: boasts, vibrant, rich in a figurative sense, seamless, robust, powerful, comprehensive, cutting-edge, nestled in, renowned, must-see, stunning. French: riche, dynamique, incontournable, de pointe, puissant, complet, fluide, niché au cœur de.

Replace the adjective with the behaviour it is standing in for, or remove it.

## Vague attribution

Triggers: experts believe, industry reports show, observers note, some critics argue, several sources. French: les experts estiment, plusieurs sources, certains estiment, il est généralement admis.

Name the source and date, or drop the claim. In review, cite the file, rule, or discussion.

## Formulaic structure

Remove generic challenge, future-outlook, or conclusion sections that merely restate the body. Keep and relocate any fact they contain.

## Vocabulary and rhythm

Watch clusters of moreover, furthermore, delve into, leverage, crucial, pivotal, landscape, tapestry, testament, showcase, underscore, invaluable, seamless, align with, foster, robust. French: par ailleurs, en outre, il convient de noter, approfondir, tirer parti, crucial, essentiel, pertinent, incontournable, en phase avec.

One word may fit. A cluster is the tell. Also simplify copula avoidance such as “serves as” and “represents”, avoid false ranges, use one hedge when uncertainty is real, and vary three consecutive sentences with the same shape.

## Chat residue and filler

Remove generic openers and closers such as “Certainly”, “Great question”, “I hope this helps”, “Bien sûr”, “Excellente question”, and “N'hésitez pas” when they add no information. Remove decorative bold labels, emoji headings, and filler such as “for the purpose of” or “it is worth noting that”.

Keep technical terms consistent: renaming the same endpoint or resource across sentences is less clear than repetition.
